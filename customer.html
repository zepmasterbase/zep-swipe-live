Payment information

Understand the direct-to-student payment process.

1. Agree on the price
Confirm the total price, deliverables and timing with the student before paying.
2. Pay the student directly
Use payment instructions provided through a verified booking workflow. Never send money to an account just because someone claims to represent Zep Swipe.
3. Confirm the payment and work
Keep your payment reference and confirm receipt with the student. A customer-reported payment is not the same as independently verified payment.

No real payment details, bank accounts or payment links are connected in this file.
